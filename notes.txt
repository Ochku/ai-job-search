As Junior Data Scientist 

HR Dyflexis Automation
Pulls registered and scheduled hours from the Dyflexis Business API, enriches them with HR reference data, and applies a library of rule-based checks.
Splits the resulting flags by responsible checker, sends Outlook emails through Microsoft Graph, and stores auditable outputs and log files.
Built for weekly or period-based controls following the 4-4-5 salary calendar used at Sawiday.

End-to-end pipeline: Fetch, clean, flag, and notify in a single run (hr_dyflexis/pipeline.py).
Rich anomaly catalog: Detects missing registrations, break-rule violations, overtime outliers, parental leave anomalies, contract mismatches, holiday work, and more.
Dynamic routing: Maps each flag to the correct checker based on location/department responsibility and falls back to HR by default.
Auditable outputs: Persists formatted logs and optional CSV exports, plus Dyflexis deep links for manual follow-up.

Transport costs
Before the project there was no idea of the transport costs for shipments before they took place. There are 4 differen transporters with each their own cost structure. Two parcel based with surcharges when specific dimensions exceeded, one pallet based for large shipments making it a vector based prediction problem as costs differ per combination, and one that has costs based on the volumetric mass. With limited data on dimensions mainly focussed on the end consumer extensive analysis was needed to find the tipping points for the model. Futher discussions with the warehouse team made for further rules to improve the model. I am currently working on this. The impact is yet to be shown.
